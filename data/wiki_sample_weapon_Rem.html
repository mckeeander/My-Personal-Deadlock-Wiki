Notes

  • While the ingame tooltip claims that Long Night is a Burst Fire, the weapon is actually a Rapid Fire gun.
  • Long Night has higher than average bullet size, at 10 inch radius. This makes aiming with Long Night very forgiving. The random pathing of the projectile VFX, and small size of the projectiles compared to the actual hitbox, give the impression that the bullets are "homing" towards a target.
